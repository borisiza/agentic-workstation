## Findings

Epic-1-specific:

- **`config/local.env` is sourced as shell code** (`scripts/doctor.sh`, `resolve_and_validate_role`), so its contents execute with the user's privileges; mitigated only by the `0600` permission that `doctor.sh` itself checks.
  Source: `deferred-work.md`, story 1.2 entry. Disposition: **defer**, tracked.
- **Config-sourcing error visibility diverges** between `doctor.sh` and its siblings — full finding and disposition in `epic-3-retro-2026-09-11.md`.

Cross-cutting findings that originate here (self-test share of each script, the
six-way duplicated assertion harness) are recorded in the epic-3 document.

five sibling scripts' self-tests; role resolution honours the
file → env → flag precedence; `gitleaks` reports zero findings over the full
history; the pre-commit hook blocks and redacts a staged private key; the six
`.gitignore` probe files stay untracked; CI `hygiene` green on every PR.

Not verified: enrolling an actual Linux, macOS or WSL2 host — the guides are
documentation and were reviewed for correctness, not executed against real
machines other than this one.
